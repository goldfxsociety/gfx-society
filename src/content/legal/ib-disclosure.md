## Short disclosure (next to every broker link or button)
> **Disclosure:** GFX may earn a commission or rebate from partner brokers when you open an account or trade through our links. You can use any broker you choose. Education only, not financial advice.

## Full disclosure
**1. Our relationship.** GoldFX Society (GFX) is an independent education community operated by its founder. The founder is an Introducing Broker (IB) affiliate of ACCM, our current partner broker. GFX is not a broker and does not hold client funds, execute trades, or manage accounts.

**2. How we get paid.** GFX may earn a commission or rebate from partner brokers when you open an account or trade through our links. The amount usually depends on how much you trade. This means GFX benefits financially when referred members trade more — which is why we teach demo first, a maximum of 1% risk per trade, and never encourage over-trading.

**3. Costs.** Spreads, commissions and fees are set by the broker and vary by account type and market conditions. Check the broker's current pricing before you open an account.

**4. Broker status.** GFX does not vouch for any broker's licensing, solvency, or execution quality. Check the broker's licensing for your country, and which legal entity you are signing up with, before opening an account or depositing.

**5. Your choice.** You are never required to use our links or a partner broker to access GFX education or the community. You can use any broker you choose, or not trade at all. Our education content is not changed by these relationships.

**6. Data.** We do not give your personal data to brokers unless you separately consent. A broker may tell us that an account was opened through our link, for example an account or client ID and trading volume, so that rebates can be calculated.

**7. Changes.** Partnerships may change; this page lists current partners and will be updated when they do.
